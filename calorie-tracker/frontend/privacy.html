Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Food Reader is a personal nutrition and wellness application. It can connect to Oura through OAuth 2.0 to combine Oura health and activity data with information stored in Food Reader.

Data we process

Depending on the features you use, Food Reader may process meal records, nutrition estimates, profile information, body measurements, and Oura data such as daily sleep, activity, readiness, stress/recovery signals and workout summaries.

Oura authorization

Oura access is granted only after you explicitly authorize Food Reader. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted in the Food Reader database. Food Reader never asks you to provide your Oura password.

How the data is used

Connected data is used to show your own dashboards, trends, comparisons and health insights. Food Reader does not sell Oura data or use it for advertising.

AI features

If you explicitly use the AI Assistant or Health Coach, relevant Food Reader data may be sent to the configured OpenAI service to generate the requested response. Oura OAuth credentials and access or refresh tokens are not sent to the AI model.

Storage and deletion

Food Reader stores imported data in its application database. Disconnecting Oura from Food Reader removes the stored Oura OAuth connection and imported Oura records for that Food Reader account. You can also stop future access by revoking the application's authorization in Oura.

Security

Food Reader uses HTTPS for its public site and encrypts stored Oura OAuth credentials. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so access to the hosting environment and application account should be protected appropriately.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact email registered for the Food Reader application in the Oura developer portal.
